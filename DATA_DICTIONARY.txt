PTopo-STAR data dictionary
Version 1.0.0

Units: stress and peak stress are MPa; dissipated energy density is MJ/m^3.
Strain is dimensionless. NRMSE and normalized distance/disagreement are dimensionless.
seed: encoder seed; held_out_family: 0-based primitive index; shots: 0,1,2,4;
repeat: support draw; sample_id: unchanged upstream record identifier;
gate: coefficient applied to the augmented-minus-explicit prediction difference.
normalizer_sd: reporting-scale standard deviation used in the paper.
